7 · Generate & Download

Processing…
The photo keeps its exact ratio. To get under the limit, quality is lowered first, then pixels are reduced evenly if needed. To reach a minimum, quality is raised first, then harmless padding is added (JPG, PNG, PDF).
Each file keeps its original name and the exact preset size (e.g. 3.5 × 4.5 cm), even with the name/date strip. "Original format" keeps JPG as JPG and PNG as PNG. You can also download one photo with the ⬇ on its thumbnail.
